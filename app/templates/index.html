A lighter footprint

Let the place stay wild.

The best journeys make room for wildlife, respect local knowledge and leave the landscape as you found it.

  • Carry reusable bottles and take your waste back with you.
  • Keep a respectful distance from wildlife and stay on marked trails.
  • Choose local guides, stays and services where possible.
  • Follow site rules and seasonal closures.
